GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any social-media video?

No. A platform link is not automatically a permitted download link. Use official download/export features or direct links that the content owner has authorized.

Why is my source not accepted?

The server only accepts exact hostnames listed in its ALLOWED_DOWNLOAD_HOSTS setting. The site owner must review and configure a permitted direct-download host first.

Is ClipNest free?

The planned model is free access supported by advertising. Advertising will be added after the site has its own privacy information and meets the advertising provider's requirements.
